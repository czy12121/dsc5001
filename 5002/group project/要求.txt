Students will work in groups of 5-8 to investigate a meaningful real-world question using a dataset. Group members must be confirmed by 1 October 2026. The assessment includes a written group report and a final presentation. Each group will have a 12-minute presentation. The group report must be submitted to Canvas by 5 December 2026 (Saturday of Week 13). The report should follow a concise research-paper structure (Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion), with a maximum length of 8 A4 pages, including figures and tables. It should include approximately 6–10 relevant visualizations. References and an Author Contribution Statement are allowed up to an additional 2 pages. Generative AI is allowed with disclosure.
